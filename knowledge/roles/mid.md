# Mid Lane

## Expected Role
Mid Lane

## Important Metrics
- CS per minute
- Kill participation
- Damage share
- Deaths
- Roam impact
- Gold diff at 14 minutes

## Metrics That Matter Less
- Vision score (matters, but lower priority than for Support)
- Tank/damage-mitigation stats (rarely the mid laner's job)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Poor wave management before roaming (leaving a bad wave state behind)
- Missing roam windows (freeze/slow push opportunities not converted into pressure)
- Overextending without vision
- Ignoring side-lane pressure while grouping/roaming
- Dying alone in the river or on a roam without backup

## General Improvement Categories
- Wave management
- Roaming and map pressure
- Lane trading
- Teamfight positioning
- Objective timing
