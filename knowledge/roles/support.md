# Support

## Expected Role
Support (Bot Lane, Utility position)

## Important Metrics
- Vision score
- Kill participation
- Roam timing
- Damage to objectives (for engage/utility supports with objective control tools)
- Assist count

## Metrics That Matter Less
- CS per minute (usually minimal to zero by design)
- Raw damage to champions (varies widely by support archetype — enchanters will always be low here)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Poor ward placement or timing (wards down late, or in low-value spots)
- Over-engaging without follow-up from the team
- Under-warding objectives ahead of dragon/baron spawns
- Roaming too early and leaving bot lane exposed to a dive
- Missing peel for carries in teamfights

## General Improvement Categories
- Vision control
- Engage/disengage timing
- Objective setup
- Teamfight positioning and peel
