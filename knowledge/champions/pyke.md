# Pyke

## Expected Role
Support (Bot Lane). Occasionally roams heavily from lane; not a farming role.

## Important Metrics
- Kill participation
- Vision score
- Roam timing / map pressure
- Gold efficiency (Pyke gives bonus gold to allies on takedowns — assists matter more than kills)

## Metrics That Matter Less
- CS per minute (Pyke intentionally farms very little by design — a low CS/min here is expected, not a mistake)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Landing Bone Skewer (Q) hooks without follow-up ready, wasting the engage
- Over-roaming and losing bot lane tempo/pressure
- Poor vision control around river and objectives
- Dying pre-6 without accounting for the lack of a Death From Below (R) execute reset
- Missing execute resets on Death From Below (R) that were available

## General Improvement Categories
- Roam timing and map pressure
- Vision control
- Engage/hook decision-making
- Objective setup (vision and pathing ahead of dragon/baron spawns)
