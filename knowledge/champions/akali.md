# Akali

## Expected Role
Mid Lane

## Important Metrics
- Kill participation (roam-heavy champion)
- CS per minute, especially pre-6
- Damage share
- Deaths
- Gold efficiency

## Metrics That Matter Less
- Raw vision score (Akali's map impact shows up in roams/kills, not wards)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Overcommitting with Twilight Shroud (W) without a clear escape route
- Missing roam windows after pushing the wave
- Poor mana management pre-6, leading to weak trades
- Dying to burst combos before securing a kill (misjudging all-in trades)

## General Improvement Categories
- Roam timing and shot-calling
- Trading patterns pre- and post-6
- Teamfight positioning
- Wave management before roaming
