# Fizz — Draft Profile

## Roles
- Middle

## Damage Type
Magic

## Scaling Profile
Mid Game

## Strengths
- High single-target burst from Seastone Trident procs
- Playful/Trickster provides a dodge, gap-close, and disengage in one tool
- Strong side-lane playmaking once mobile enough to roam safely

## Weaknesses
- Short-ranged and reliant on landing an all-in to win a trade
- Vulnerable if Playful/Trickster is baited out early or mistimed
- Limited teamfight utility outside of diving a single backline target

## Preferred Playstyle
Skirmisher who fishes for short trades to stack Seastone Trident, then
looks for an all-in window on a cooldown or positioning mistake rather
than a standard extended lane trade.

## Common Counters
- Poppy
- Lissandra
- Galio

## Favorable Matchups
- Xerath
- Zoe
- Anivia

## Synergy Champions
- Nautilus
- Amumu
- Sejuani
