# Yone — Draft Profile

## Roles
- Middle
- Top

## Damage Type
Hybrid

## Scaling Profile
Scales Throughout

## Strengths
- Strong extended-trade duelist thanks to the double-crit passive
- High single-target mobility from repeated E dashes
- Ultimate provides both a teamfight-swinging engage and a knock-up follow-up

## Weaknesses
- Committing E forward leaves him without an escape until it resets
- Vulnerable to hard crowd control landed before he can dash away
- Falls off relative to hypercarries if he loses his early lead

## Preferred Playstyle
Aggressive skirmisher who looks for short, sharp trades in lane, then uses
his mobility to pick off overextended targets and snowball into a
teamfight-swinging ultimate.

## Common Counters
- Malphite
- Poppy
- Renekton

## Favorable Matchups
- Ryze
- Azir
- Viktor

## Synergy Champions
- Nautilus
- Leona
- Sejuani
