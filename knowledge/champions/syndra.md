# Syndra

## Expected Role
Mid Lane

## Important Metrics
- CS per minute
- Damage share
- Kill participation (stun/lockdown enables teamfight picks)
- Deaths
- Gold diff at 14 minutes

## Metrics That Matter Less
- Raw vision score (relevant but secondary to farm/positioning for a control mage)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Poor Dark Sphere (Q) management — wasting spheres instead of stacking for Unleashed Power (R) stun setups
- Overextending due to lack of a mobility/escape tool
- Losing wave control, getting pushed out of experience/gold range
- Weak-side positioning in teamfights (front-lining or getting caught by flanks)

## General Improvement Categories
- Wave management
- Positioning in and around fights
- Spell/resource (sphere) management
- Teamfight target prioritization
