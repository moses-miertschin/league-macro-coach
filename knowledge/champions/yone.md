# Yone

## Expected Role
Mid Lane (secondary: Top Lane)

## Important Metrics
- CS per minute (needs steady farm to scale into fights)
- Kill participation
- Deaths (especially post-engage deaths)
- Damage share
- Gold diff at 14 minutes

## Metrics That Matter Less
- Vision score (moderate importance, not a primary role for Yone)
- Assist count in isolation (Yone is a damage/carry threat, not a peel/utility champion)

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Overextending immediately after landing a double-knockup or all-in ("tunnel vision" chasing a kill)
- Forcing fights without checking minion wave state, leaving the wave to push into him
- Using Soul Unbound (R) offensively without a clear disengage plan
- Getting caught out of position while recalling or backing
- Itemizing incorrectly into heavy-armor/tank compositions

## General Improvement Categories
- Wave management before committing to fights
- Trading patterns in lane
- Positioning during and after all-ins
- Objective timing and grouping
