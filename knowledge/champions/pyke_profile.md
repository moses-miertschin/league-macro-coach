# Pyke — Draft Profile

## Roles
- Utility

## Damage Type
Physical

## Scaling Profile
Early Game

## Strengths
- Bone Skewer hooks create engage opportunities from unusual angles
- Death From Below grants a low-health execute with a reset on takedowns
- Shares bonus gold with allies on takedowns, accelerating the whole bot lane

## Weaknesses
- Very low base health pool for a champion that plays on the front line
- Intentionally low CS/min by design, limiting his own item power
- No reliable follow-up if a hook whiffs or lands without support nearby

## Preferred Playstyle
Roam-heavy engage support who looks for hook picks and execute resets
rather than a standard peel/farm support lane, converting kills into
tempo and gold for the rest of the team.

## Common Counters
- Leona
- Blitzcrank
- Nautilus

## Favorable Matchups
- Soraka
- Janna
- Lulu

## Synergy Champions
- Draven
- Miss Fortune
- Kai'Sa
