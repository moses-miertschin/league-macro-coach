# Syndra — Draft Profile

## Roles
- Middle

## Damage Type
Magic

## Scaling Profile
Late Game

## Strengths
- Long-range poke and burst once Dark Spheres are stacked
- Unleashed Power provides a long, hard-lockdown stun for teamfight picks
- Strong wave-clear and siege pressure in the mid-to-late game

## Weaknesses
- No mobility or escape tool, making her vulnerable to dive/gap-close threats
- Sphere management requires setup time before her burst combo is available
- Falls off in lane priority if pushed out of experience/gold range

## Preferred Playstyle
Control mage who plays a patient poke-and-farm lane, stacking spheres for
her stun-into-burst combo, and looks to convert that combo into a
teamfight pick rather than an early-game skirmish.

## Common Counters
- Zed
- Talon
- LeBlanc

## Favorable Matchups
- Orianna
- Viktor
- Ryze

## Synergy Champions
- Nautilus
- Amumu
- Jarvan IV
