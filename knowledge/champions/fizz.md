# Fizz

## Expected Role
Mid Lane

## Important Metrics
- CS per minute
- Kill participation / solo kill potential
- Damage share
- Deaths from failed all-ins

## Metrics That Matter Less
- Vision score

## Common Mistakes to Detect
- Mistiming Playful/Trickster (E) — using it too early (wasted) or too late (missed the dodge/engage window)
- Overextending under enemy turret
- Poor trading pattern (fishing for trades without landing Seastone Trident procs)
- Missing playmaking windows on side lanes (Fizz's mobility is wasted if he stays static)

## General Improvement Categories
- Trading patterns in lane
- All-in timing and execution
- Roam / side-lane playmaking decisions
- Wave management
