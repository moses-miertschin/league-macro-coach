# Akali — Draft Profile

## Roles
- Middle

## Damage Type
Magic

## Scaling Profile
Early Game

## Strengths
- Strong roaming pressure and solo-kill potential from level 6 onward
- Twilight Shroud grants vision denial that enables all-ins and disengages
- High burst damage in short trading windows

## Weaknesses
- Mana-starved before level 6, limiting early trading
- Twilight Shroud can be walked out of or countered with true sight
- Loses effectiveness if she falls behind and can't reliably burst targets

## Preferred Playstyle
Roam-heavy assassin who trades cautiously pre-6, then uses kill pressure
and shroud-enabled all-ins to snowball through roams and picks rather
than a standard farm-and-scale lane.

## Common Counters
- Lissandra
- Kassadin
- Xerath

## Favorable Matchups
- Orianna
- Viktor
- Malzahar

## Synergy Champions
- Elise
- Jarvan IV
- Lee Sin
