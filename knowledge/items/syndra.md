# Syndra — Item Guidance

## Core Build
- Liandry's Torment
- Sorcerer's Shoes

## Banshee's Veil
- Valuable when: enemy has 2+ champions dealing primarily magic damage
- Bad when: enemy team is almost entirely physical damage
- Champion fit: spell shield protects Syndra's positioning while she sets up a combo
- Priority: high vs AP-heavy comps
- Game state: situational regardless of ahead/behind

## Morellonomicon
- Valuable when: enemy has healing or sustain in their kit
- Bad when: enemy has no meaningful healing
- Champion fit: fits naturally into the mage damage build, no wasted stats
- Priority: medium vs sustain-heavy champions
- Game state: situational

## Zhonya's Hourglass
- Valuable when: enemy has 2+ champions dealing primarily physical damage, or a fed enemy carry threatens burst kills
- Bad when: ahead with no real burst/dive threat on the enemy team
- Champion fit: stasis is Syndra's main tool for surviving a dive as a stationary control mage
- Priority: high vs AD-heavy comps, when behind, or vs a fed enemy carry
- Game state: especially valuable when behind

## Rabadon's Deathcap
- Valuable when: ahead in gold or kills, want to press the advantage
- Bad when: behind or even -- delays a defensive item you may need first
- Champion fit: raw damage amplifier, converts a lead into faster kills
- Priority: high when ahead
- Game state: ahead-state item

## Avoid: Sterak's Gage
- Bad when: always for this champion
- Champion fit: poor -- built for AD bruisers/duelists, provides no ability power for Syndra
