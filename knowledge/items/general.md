# General — Fallback Item Guidance

Used when a champion doesn't have its own knowledge/items/{champion}.md, or
as a supplement for triggers a champion file doesn't cover.

## Core Build

## Wit's End
- Valuable when: enemy has 2+ champions dealing primarily magic damage
- Bad when: enemy team is almost entirely physical damage
- Champion fit: general-purpose magic resist + attack speed for AD-leaning champions
- Priority: medium vs AP-heavy comps
- Game state: situational regardless of ahead/behind

## Executioner's Calling
- Valuable when: enemy has healing or sustain in their kit
- Bad when: enemy has no meaningful healing
- Champion fit: cheap, generally fits any damage-dealing champion
- Priority: medium vs sustain-heavy champions
- Game state: situational

## Zhonya's Hourglass
- Valuable when: behind, or facing a fed enemy carry that threatens burst kills
- Bad when: ahead with no real burst threat on the enemy team
- Champion fit: general-purpose defensive item for any squishy damage dealer
- Priority: high when behind or vs a fed enemy carry
- Game state: behind-state / defensive item

## Youmuu's Ghostblade
- Valuable when: ahead in gold or kills, want to press the advantage
- Bad when: behind or even -- active upside is wasted without a lead
- Champion fit: generic mobility/snowball item for skirmish-oriented champions
- Priority: medium when ahead
- Game state: ahead-state item
