# Yone — Item Guidance

## Core Build
- Kraken Slayer
- Berserker's Greaves

## Wit's End
- Valuable when: enemy has 2+ champions dealing primarily magic damage
- Bad when: enemy team is almost entirely physical damage
- Champion fit: attack-speed scaling synergizes with Yone's W stacks
- Priority: high vs AP-heavy comps
- Game state: situational regardless of ahead/behind

## Death's Dance
- Valuable when: enemy has 2+ champions dealing primarily physical damage
- Bad when: enemy damage is mostly burst/magic -- healing doesn't offset burst well
- Champion fit: extends Yone's ability to stay in extended fights
- Priority: high vs AD-heavy comps
- Game state: especially valuable when behind

## Executioner's Calling
- Valuable when: enemy has healing or sustain in their kit
- Bad when: enemy has no meaningful healing
- Champion fit: cheap early grievous wounds source that doesn't derail the core build
- Priority: medium vs sustain-heavy champions
- Game state: situational

## Maw of Malmortius
- Valuable when: facing a fed enemy carry that threatens burst kills
- Bad when: no enemy champion is credibly threatening a burst kill
- Champion fit: spell shield punishes all-ins and protects Yone's dive window
- Priority: high vs a fed enemy carry
- Game state: situational, most valuable when behind

## Youmuu's Ghostblade
- Valuable when: ahead in gold or kills, want to press the advantage
- Bad when: behind or even -- active upside is wasted without a lead
- Champion fit: pairs with Yone's dash-reset mobility
- Priority: high when ahead
- Game state: ahead-state item

## Zhonya's Hourglass
- Valuable when: behind, or facing a fed enemy carry that threatens burst kills
- Bad when: ahead with no real burst threat on the enemy team
- Champion fit: general-purpose defensive item, buys time to scale back into the game
- Priority: high when behind or vs a fed enemy carry
- Game state: behind-state / defensive item

## Avoid: Guardian Angel
- Bad when: always for this champion
- Champion fit: poor -- Yone lacks reliable disengage to use the second life well
